About

I am a fifth-year Integrated MSc student at the University of Hyderabad. My work covers genomics, transcriptomics, flux balance analysis and machine learning for biological data, alongside hands-on experience in molecular biology, protein expression and microbial culture.

I want to connect computational analysis with experimental validation so that predictions about complex systems, such as cancer, can be tested in the lab.

Research interests: cancer genomics, systems biology, bioinformatics and computational biology, machine learning in biomedical research, mathematical modelling of biological systems.
