Research Projects

### 1. Cape Vulture Transcriptome Analysis
**Research Focus:** Investigating intron retention in the CYP2C19 transcript of Cape vulture (*Gyps coprotheres*) and its potential association with diclofenac sensitivity.

- Transcriptome assembly using Trinity and RNA-SPAdes.
- Quality assessment using FastQC, MultiQC and BUSCO.
- Transcript annotation and coding sequence prediction.
- Reference-based alignment using HISAT2.
- Investigation of CYP2C19 transcript structure and intron retention.

### 2. Parkinson's Disease and Gut Microbiome
**Research Focus:** Exploring common gut microbial taxa and their associated functional pathways across Parkinson's disease, healthy controls, and prodromal cohorts.

- Comparative microbiome analysis across different cohorts.
- Identification of shared microbial taxa.
- Investigation of microbial functional pathways.
- Exploring potential microbial biomarkers for early disease prediction.

### 3. Mathematical Modelling of Biological Systems
Research Focus: Understanding biological processes through mathematical and computational modelling.

- Ordinary Differential Equation (ODE) modelling.
- Predator–prey systems and ecological models.
- Gene regulatory networks.
- p53–Mdm2 signalling pathways.
- Bifurcation and parameter estimation analysis.
